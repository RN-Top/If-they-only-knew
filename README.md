# If They Only Knew

"If you only knew the magnificence of the 3, 6 and 9." (attributed to Nikola Tesla; original source unverified)

This project treats that line as a hypothesis and tests it. Every claim gets a clear statement, a test anyone can rerun, and a verdict: proven, disproven or open. Control numbers are always tested alongside 3, 6 and 9, so the results can't just be pattern-hunting.

## Lenses
- **The Family**: every multiple of 3 reduces to 3, 6 or 9 (digital roots, base 10)
- **Doubling Circuit**: doubling from 1 cycles 1-2-4-8-7-5 and never touches 3, 6 or 9
- **Nine-Box Grid**: the 8 magic squares hidden among 362,880 arrangements
- **As Above, So Below**: earth and sky measurements tested against chance
